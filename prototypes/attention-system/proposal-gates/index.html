SituationRoute

No action changes, evidence is weak, or the content is routine, private, secret, or duplicativeStay silent

Hard limits

Only event-triggered routing. At most three open gates per project, one gate per source, and no re-surface for 24 hours without new evidence. The decision pass may use the current task context, one search of at most five results, and one explicit source open. It never wakes an agent, starts a job, polls with an LLM, or treats historical session IDs as live.
